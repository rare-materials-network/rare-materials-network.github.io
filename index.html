Responsible and Resilient Electronic and Electromagnetic Materials

Funded by

Materials for electronics that last, and can be sourced responsibly.

The RaRE Materials Network+ connects the UK community working on electronic and electromagnetic materials.

The network brings together researchers across engineering, chemistry, physics and the social sciences with industry, RTOs, and policy partners. We will delivery scientific activities, co-development sandpits, and fund feasibility projects and exchanges. Collectively, the RaRE Network+ will build a shared evidence base for the advanced electronic materials sector.

Responsible

Lower impact materials and processes, designed for reuse, repair and recovery.

Resilient

Enabling a secure supply chains through novel devices, materials, and circularity.

Electromagnetic performance

Focusing on application-agnostic material properties, enabling critical technologies from telecoms, power, to quantum.
